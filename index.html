No storefront checkout drama — just browse, message, and pay. Here's exactly how it works.

Our story

An Instagram-built crop top studio

Cinnamon Restyle is run by Solea Styles as a small, Instagram-first shop — hand-picking graphic tees and basics, restyling them into fitted crop pieces, and photographing every one on our signature mannequin setup before it's posted for sale.

With 660+ pieces restyled and a growing community on Instagram, we ship every order pan-India, and keep the process simple: browse, DM, pay, and it's on its way.

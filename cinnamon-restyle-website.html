How it works

From worn to worn-again

Every restyle follows the same honest process — no guesswork, no surprises.

01

Send or drop off

Bring in the garment you want reimagined, or ship it to our studio with a quick note on what you're picturing.

02

Design consult

We talk fit, fabric, and direction — silhouette changes, dye work, patchwork, or full reconstruction.

03

Hand restyled

Your piece is cut, sewn, and finished by hand in our studio, with progress photos along the way.

04

Delivered ready to wear

Packaged and shipped (or ready for pickup), finished with care instructions for its new life.

Our story

Slow fashion, stitched by hand

Cinnamon Restyle started as a way to keep beloved clothes out of the landfill — one denim jacket, one thrifted dress at a time. Today we run a small studio dedicated to restyling, redyeing, and reconstructing garments into pieces people are excited to wear again.

Every order is handled in-house, from consultation to final stitch, with an eye for fit, fabric integrity, and a little bit of unexpected detail.

Sustainably sourced

We prioritize deadstock, thrifted, and client-supplied fabric.

Made to order

No mass production — every piece is cut for its future owner.

Hand-finished

Stitching, dye work, and detailing done entirely in-studio.

Honest pricing

Transparent quotes before any cut is made.
